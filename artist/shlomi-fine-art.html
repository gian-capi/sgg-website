Shlomi Fine Art

From a young age, HStudio founder Schlomi Haziza was declaring his presence in the art world. Coming from a family of artists who cultivated his confidence to pursue his passion, Haziza started as a painter and later began to explore new mediums. Running a small studio in his hometown launched his journey to Los Angeles, which in turn, manifested a leading art and contemporary design studio.

Acrylic is the most sophisticated material Haziza has mastered to date. He was one of the first innovators of this unforgiving medium, and now is a world leader in acrylic art and design.
