Will Bullas

With the encouragement of his wife, Claudia, he then quit his printing press job—reproducing the work of other artists—and concentrated on his own art. The couple settled in California, where Will's exclusive relationship with galleries resulted in a growing group of dedicated, enthusiastic collectors. Will makes fine art fun. This master of one-liners combines award winning artist skills with a hilarious point of view, creating a refreshing and unexpected new way to enjoy fine art. The Infused Metal process represents a new art medium.
