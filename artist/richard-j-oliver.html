Richard J. Oliver

Richard J Oliver Born in Pontypridd, Wales in 1975. Holds an honorary degree in Fine Art from the University Of West of England and undertaking a Masters in Fine Art UWIC Wales.

Currently Paints and resides in Los Angeles CA.

Solo exhibitions include the Known Gallery, Hollywood, Los Angeles. Museum of Modern Art, Wales. Attic Gallery, Swansea and Rhondda Heritage park permanent Mural. Mixed Exhibitions Worldwide including London, New York and most recently Los Angeles.
