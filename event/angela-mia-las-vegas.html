Angela Mia Art Show in Las Vegas

  • Friday, October 9th, 1pm – 6pm
  • Saturday, October 10th, 1pm – 6pm

Discover the elegance and emotion of Angela Mia's figurative bronze sculptures at Wyland Galleries Las Vegas, on view October 9–10 for this special two-day appearance.

Featuring

Angela Mia

Since sculpting her first bronze in 1994, Angela Mia has been creating figurative bronze sculptures for international collectors, corporations and institutions. She lives and works in Texas. Exalting the human spirit through expressive faces and natural body movement has become Angela’s recognized trademark.
